關於這個實習About the internship

實習由臺北醫學大學醫學資訊研究所黃芝瑋助理教授指導，學員在醫學科技學院健康資訊科技國際研究中心（ICHIT）進行研究。我們的工作橫跨臨床醫學、資料科學與健康科技：不只做模型，也追問這個工具在臨床上是否有效、病人與醫護是否真的會用，以及要如何安全地走向實際應用。

The internship is supervised by Dr. Chih-Wei (Grace) Huang, Assistant Professor at the Graduate Institute of Biomedical Informatics, Taipei Medical University (TMU). Interns are based at the International Center for Health Information Technology (ICHIT), College of Medical Science and Technology. Our work sits where clinical medicine, data science, and health technology meet. We do not stop at building models: we ask whether a tool is clinically valid, whether patients and clinicians will actually use it, and what it takes to bring it into care safely.

每位學員會在第一週和老師確認一個明確的專題，實習結束時帶走一份具體成果。

Every intern agrees on one well-defined project in the first week and leaves with a concrete output.

研究方向Research areas

依你的背景與興趣，加入其中一個方向。You join one area, matched to your background and interests.

01真實世界資料的臨床 AIClinical AI from real-world data

使用臺北醫學大學臨床研究資料庫（TMUCRD），串接北醫附醫、萬芳、雙和三院的電子病歷，開發與驗證腎臟病、血液透析、癌症及住院風險預測模型。

Build and validate prediction models on the TMU Clinical Research Database (TMUCRD), which links EHRs from three affiliated hospitals. Topics include kidney disease, hemodialysis, oncology, and inpatient risk.

適合：Good fit: 會 Python 或 RPython or R

02生成式 AI 病人衛教Generative AI for patient education

把臨床指引轉成準確、易懂的病人回答：建置知識庫與檢索增強生成（RAG）系統，目前以慢性腎臟病與透析照護為主，並評估大型語言模型輸出的正確性、安全性與可讀性。

Turn clinical guidelines into accurate, patient-friendly answers. Build knowledge bases and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems, currently focused on kidney disease and dialysis, and evaluate LLM outputs for accuracy, safety, and readability.

適合：Good fit: 程式或醫療背景皆可Programming or clinical background

03數位健康介入Digital health interventions

為慢性病病人與癌症存活者設計並測試自我管理工具，從需求評估、以使用者為中心的設計，到臨床試驗。

Design and test tools that help people with chronic disease and cancer survivors manage their health, from needs assessment and user-centered design to clinical trials.

適合：Good fit: 護理、公衛、醫療、設計Nursing, public health, medicine, design

04醫材軟體法規與轉譯Regulation and translation

了解 AI 工具如何成為醫療器材軟體（SaMD），以及研究成果如何透過醫院與新創走向產品。

Learn how an AI tool becomes Software as a Medical Device (SaMD), and how research moves into products through hospitals and startups.

適合：Good fit: 管理、法規、醫療背景Business, regulatory, clinical

常見問題FAQ

我沒有程式背景，可以申請嗎？I don't code. Can I still apply?

可以。數位健康介入與法規轉譯方向不需要程式能力，醫療、護理、公衛與管理背景都很適合。

Yes. The digital health intervention and regulation tracks do not require programming. Clinical, nursing, public health, and business backgrounds fit well.

可以線上實習嗎？Can I do the internship online?

TEEP 獎學金以來臺實習為原則。若你只能線上參與，請先來信討論。

The TEEP scholarship is for onsite internships in Taiwan. If you can only join remotely, email us first to discuss.

已經畢業可以申請嗎？I have already graduated. Can I apply?

TEEP 以在學學生為主。已畢業者請來信說明你的情況，我們會協助確認是否符合資格。

TEEP is mainly for enrolled students. If you have graduated, email us with your situation and we will check whether you are eligible.

實習結束後可以繼續讀研究所嗎？Can I continue to graduate study afterwards?

可以，而且我們很歡迎。已有實習生在結束後申請進入北醫醫學資訊研究所碩士班。

Yes, and we encourage it. Former interns have gone on to join the MSc program in Biomedical Informatics at TMU.
